Practice lab

Four network CLIs, right in your browser.

Juniper Junos, Cisco IOS XR on an ASR 9000, IOS on an ME 3600X metro Ethernet switch, and a Catalyst access switch. Type real commands, change the config, save it, break it and fix it, or take a quiz. Built for interview practice.

Simulated. These are pretend devices written in JavaScript. Nothing here connects to a real router, and nothing you type leaves your browser. Addresses are documentation and private ranges; the hostnames and the network are made up.

How it works

Saving

Each device keeps its own config in this browser's local storage, the way the real one would: Junos when you commit, IOS XR when you commit, and the two IOS switches when you write memory. Reload this page and the Cisco boxes come back with their startup config, so anything you didn't save is gone. Reset to factory starts a device over.

What's real and what isn't

The command syntax, the modes and the error messages follow the real platforms closely. The outputs are rebuilt from your config plus a small pretend network around each box: neighbors, customers, a transit provider. Show commands answer to what you configure, but there's no real routing or forwarding underneath, and only a slice of each CLI is here.

Quizzes

Each lab has three: command syntax, config tasks, and troubleshooting, where something is broken and you find and fix it. Every question runs on a scratch copy of the device, so your saved lab stays put. Abbreviations count. Your best score per quiz stays in this browser too.
